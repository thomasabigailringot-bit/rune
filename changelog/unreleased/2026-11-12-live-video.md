- **`LiveVideo`: a video the app feeds with H.264 access units and AAC frames, on Windows.** The app demuxes (HLS, RTMP, whatever it reads) and pushes `AccessUnit`s and `AacFrame`s; Rune decodes them with Media Foundation's H.264 and AAC transforms (the GPU's decoder where there is one), keeps a jitter buffer of `LiveConfig::target_delay` (80 ms by default) and runs the clock from the sound device, resampling the sound to run a little fast for a backlog. `MediaSource` gains a `Live` variant, so an exhaustive `match` on it needs an arm. `Player::stats` returns `PlayerStats` (pictures shown, dropped, late, stalls, sound gaps, queue depth, push-to-present latency, A/V offset) and `Player::set_picture_observer` calls back with each picture's time and presentation instant, on Windows (zeros and no calls elsewhere). The Windows sound output takes raw PCM and follows a clock that runs off 1x without going silent.
