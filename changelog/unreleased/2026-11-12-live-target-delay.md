- **`LiveVideo::set_target_delay` and `target_delay`: the jitter buffer can change while playing.** The clock moves to the new delay by its usual steering (a little slower to fill a raised one, a little faster to drain a lowered one). A live video whose window does not draw no longer queues the stream without limit: more than the delay plus a margin of video waiting is cut at a keyframe, the dropped pictures count in `PlayerStats::late`, and the video rejoins near live when the window draws again. The clock's thresholds scale with the delay, so delays of a second or more do not jump or slow down on a stream that arrives in bursts.
