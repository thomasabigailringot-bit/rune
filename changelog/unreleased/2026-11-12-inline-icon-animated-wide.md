- **Inline image icons animate and can be wider than high.** An animated image (GIF, WebP) set with `El::inline_icon` plays like a plain picture does: it draws its frame for the clock and wakes the window for the next only while drawn in a shown window. `InlineIcon::width` gives the box a width of its own beside `size` (its height); the paragraph reserves that width, and the image fits the box, keeping its shape and centred. A square icon, with no `width`, is as before.
