- **Windows live video: the sound recovers behind a hidden window.** While nothing draws, the decoder thread starts the clock when media is ready, holds it after a stalled stream until the target delay is queued again, and restarts it after a discontinuity, so the sound neither stays silent for long after a stall nor never starts. The window takes the clock as it is when it draws again, and the decoder reads the sound between slices of decoding on the way back. A new texture ring after a resolution change keeps freeing the textures of dropped pictures.
