- **Windows live video: the sound stays on the stream behind a minimized or covered window.** The decoder thread now steers a `LiveVideo`'s clock (speed, and a jump when it falls far behind) whether or not a frame draws, decodes no pictures while nothing draws, and restarts from the keyframe before the clock when a frame draws again. A picture dropped before it reached the screen frees its texture at once, so the texture ring no longer fills up and stops the video for good.
